About

I am a computational condensed matter physicist specializing in first-principles simulations, density functional theory (DFT) and many-body perturbation theory, with a focus on the electronic and optical properties of low-dimensional and nanostructured materials.

I have experience with GW and Bethe–Salpeter equation (BSE) calculations for quasiparticle electronic structure, excitonic effects, optical absorption and dielectric screening. My work covers two-dimensional materials, carbon-based nanostructures, boron nitride nanotubes, penta-graphene, graphene derivatives, strain engineering and computational nanomechanics.
